# Image-to-Video: Voices Only (No Background Music)

## Audio Directive

Generate video with **character voices and dialogue ONLY**. No background music, no ambient sounds, no sound effects. Purely vocal audio.

## What to Include

- Character speech with clear enunciation
- Lip-sync matching spoken words
- Natural voice tone and emotion
- Dialogue timing aligned with action

## What to Exclude

- NO background music
- NO ambient noise (wind, traffic, crowds)
- NO sound effects (footsteps, doors, impacts)
- NO musical scoring or soundtrack
- Mute all non-dialogue audio

## Prompting Pattern

When describing dialogue, use this structure:
- Character says "[exact words]" with [tone/emotion]
- Include lip-sync and mouth movement
- Silent background / no music

## Negative Reinforcement

If audio still contains unwanted elements, these phrases help:
- "purely vocal audio only"
- "mute all non-dialogue sounds"
- "silent environment except speech"
- "no musical accompaniment"
